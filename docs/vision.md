# Smart Insulin Pump (SIP) — Vision Document

## Problem Statement

Managing Type 1 Diabetes (T1D) requires continuous monitoring, precise insulin adjustments, and timely decision-making.  
Many patients and caregivers struggle to track glucose levels, manage insulin pumps, and respond quickly to health changes.

**SIP** aims to simplify this process by providing a secure, intelligent, and easy-to-use mobile application for remote insulin pump management.

---

## Target Users

- Individuals with **Type 1 Diabetes (T1D)**
- **Caregivers** (parents, family members)
- **Teachers** who supervise students with T1D
- **Healthcare professionals** such as doctors or nurses

---

## Key Features

- **Secure registration and authentication**
- **User-friendly dashboard** for real-time pump data
- **Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) integration**
- **Alert notifications** for abnormal glucose levels or pump status
- **Glucose level predictions (AI-powered)**
- **Data sharing** with caregivers or medical staff

---

## What Makes It Smart

SIP incorporates AI features that analyze glucose trends and predict potential high or low glucose events.  
Based on these insights, it can provide **suggested actions** — such as adjusting insulin dosage or alerting caregivers — to support proactive, safe diabetes management.

---

## Vision Summary

SIP empowers people living with T1D to live more freely and confidently through **smart automation, real-time monitoring, and connected care** — all in one secure mobile app.
