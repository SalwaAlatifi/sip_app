## Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)

### 1. Security & Privacy
- The system shall require authentication.  
- The system shall encrypt sensitive data stored locally or in the cloud.  
- The system shall allow role-based access for caregivers and healthcare staff.  
- The system shall comply with relevant healthcare data protection standards.  

### 2. Reliability & Availability
- The system shall operate continuously and provide real-time readings.  
- The system shall tolerate temporary network outages.  
- The system shall log critical actions for traceability.  

### 3. Performance
- Real-time glucose readings shall update with minimal delay.  
- Notifications and alerts shall be delivered promptly to users and caregivers.  

### 4. Usability & Accessibility
- The system shall provide an intuitive and easy-to-use dashboard.  
- Notifications shall be customizable.  
- The system shall be accessible for users with disabilities.
