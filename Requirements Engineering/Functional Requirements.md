## Core Functionalities

### 1. Real-Time Monitoring
- The system shall display real-time glucose readings.  
- The system shall send notifications for hypo/hyperglycemic events.  
- The system shall allow caregivers to view real-time glucose readings for multiple patients.  

### 2. Insulin Management
- The system shall give bolus insulin suggestions.  
- The system shall allow the user to modify insulin dosage.  
- The system shall allow remote insulin injections.  

### 3. Data Input & Tracking
- The system shall allow users to input carbohydrate intake.  
- The system shall display historical glucose readings and logs.  
- The system shall provide trend analysis (daily, weekly, monthly).  

### 4. Integration
- The system shall connect with third-party CGM sensors.  
- The system shall connect with the insulin pump via BLE.
- The system shall displays the devices status (batter, connection state and insulin amount).

### 5. AI & Prediction
- The system AI model shall provide glucose level predictions.  

### 6. Emergency & Communication
- The system shall communicate with emergency services (997) in critical situations.  

### 7. Caregiver & Healthcare Staff Access
- The system shall allow caregivers to monitor multiple patients.  
- The system shall allow healthcare staff to access patient data and logs with appropriate permissions.  

### 8. Security & Privacy
- The system shall require authentication (password or third-party auth) for access.   
- The system shall provide role-based access control for caregivers and healthcare staff.  

### 9. Usability
- The system shall allow users to set target glucose ranges.  
- The system shall allow customization of notifications (sound, vibration, silent).
