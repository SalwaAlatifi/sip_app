# Constraints
- The project is developed and maintained by a single team member, limiting development speed and parallel task execution.
- No official medical certification (FDA/CE/SFDA) can be obtained within the project timeline, restricting the app from being used as a clinical tool.
- Dependency on third-party CGM and pump manufacturers for APIs/SDKs may limit integration capabilities and available data.
- No manufacturing capabilities limit testing with real pump hardware; testing depends on receiving a functional simulator or prototype.
- BLE connection range, signal interference, and mobile device permissions may impact pump or CGM communication reliability.
- Dependencies on third-party libraries (BLE, ML kits, Appwrite SDK, notifications) may introduce breaking changes or compatibility issues.
- No ability to develop, build, or publish an Apple version due to the lack of: a Mac device (required for iOS development & building Flutter iOS apps) and an Apple Developer Program account (required for App Store deployment).
